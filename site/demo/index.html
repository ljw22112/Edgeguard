What you are watching

Workers walk between pick stations across four forklift lanes. On the left, any forklift within the warning zone of a person slows to walking pace and stops inside the danger zone, and the person’s tag buzzes. On the right, a worker gets WAIT only when he and a forklift would meet, GO once it has passed, or STEP ASIDE when he stands in its lane. The forklift keeps its speed while it can still stop short of him.
